# Feature Specification: 032-bots-integrations (Community Chatbots & Notifications)

**Feature Branch**: `032-bots-integrations`  
**Status**: Planned  

---

## 1. Overview

Messaging apps (Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp) are widely used for daily alerts and community information in Bangladesh. Desh API Bots provide interactive chatbots and scheduled notification broadcasts powered by live API data.

---

## 2. Capabilities

1. **Daily Prayer & Ramadan Timetable Bot**: Daily morning schedule for district-specific prayer times, Sehri and Iftar countdown reminders.
2. **Dhaka Metro & Railway Query Bot**: Quick slash-command lookups for Metro Line-6 and intercity train schedules, fares, and off-days (`/train 701`, `/metro agargaon motijheel`).
3. **Severe Weather & Cyclone Alert Webhook**: Automated webhook triggers when Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) issues Maritime Warning Signals 4 or higher or Special Cyclone Bulletins.
4. **TCB Daily Price Checker**: Lookup today's essential commodity price ranges directly in chat.
